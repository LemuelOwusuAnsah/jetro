Jam highlights

Live jam sessions

Musicians from across Ghana and the diaspora link up for spontaneous, unrehearsed jams. Anything can happen — and it always does.

Jetro beats stage

Curated sets from underground R&B, afrobeats, highlife, and electronic artists. Sunset sessions & after-dark grooves.

Open jam labs

Bring your instrument or just your voice. Guided jam circles, drum workshops, and vocal freestyle sessions. All levels welcome.

Immersive sound park

After dusk, the beach becomes a glowing wonderland with projection mapping and interactive sound sculptures.

Maker's market

50+ independent creators selling vinyl, wearables, handmade instruments, and one-of-a-kind pieces.

Street food & chill

Local food trucks, craft coffee, and a cozy lounge area to recharge between jams.
